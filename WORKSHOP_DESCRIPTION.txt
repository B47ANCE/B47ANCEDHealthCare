[h1]medWorks[/h1]

Medical examination, treatment, surgery, trauma procedures, and recovery infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.

medWorks owns authoritative medical actions while consuming shared entity and anatomy capabilities where a procedure requires them.

[b]Mod Version: 0.9.2[/b]

[b]Development Status[/b]

The examination and procedure framework is implemented and actively tested; deeper treatments, presentation, and multiplayer validation remain in development.

[h2]Current Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Bounded read-only examination-provider API
[*]Vanilla human BodyDamage and BodyPart observation adapter
[*]Local wound infection kept distinct from Knox infection
[*]Timed local and remote health-panel examinations with range, movement, player-identity, and synchronized snapshot revalidation
[*]Authoritative replay-safe procedure reservations and patient-resource locks
[*]Completion-time validation and uncertain-operation recovery inspection
[*]Timed zombie-corpse part recovery through Project M.O.R.T.I.S. anatomy
[*]Server-authoritative remote recovery with exact corpse/tool reconstruction, bounded requests, and replay protection
[*]Detached-part provenance for downstream consumers
[*]Optional authoritative sunburn treatment when lightWorks is installed
[*]Admin and debug procedure diagnostics that cannot mutate uncertain state
[/list]

[h2]Ecosystem Integration[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires Project M.O.R.T.I.S. for shared entity and anatomy infrastructure
[*]Requires experienceWorks for medical knowledge and progression requirements
[*]Provides medical and veterinary procedure APIs to compatible consumers
[*]Exposes recovered body-part provenance used by Project Nightfall effigies
[*]Consumes lightWorks sunburn state only when lightWorks is installed
[/list]

[/list]
